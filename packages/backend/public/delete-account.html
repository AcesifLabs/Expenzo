Account Deletion Request

Expenzo by Acesif Labs

How to delete your account

You can request account deletion in two ways:

Via the app

  1. Open the Expenzo app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion

Via email

Send an email to support@acesiflabs.com with the subject line "Delete My Expenzo Account" from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Data that will be deleted

  • Profile information (name, email, profile photo)
  • All expense and income records
  • Categories and budgets
  • Message sources and SMS parsing rules
  • Expense templates
  • Recurring transactions and pending recurring items

Data that may be retained

  • Anonymous, aggregated analytics data not linked to your identity
  • Firebase authentication logs — managed by Google per their privacy policy
Important: Account deletion is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, all associated data is permanently erased and cannot be recovered.
